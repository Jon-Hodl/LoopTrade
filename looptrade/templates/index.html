What is LoopTrade?

LoopTrade is an open-source automated trading bot for Bitcoin. It uses grid trading strategies on LN Markets to help you buy low and sell high (or short sell high and buy low) automatically.

How It Works

  • Set your buy and sell prices for Bitcoin
  • The bot automatically places limit orders
  • When orders fill, it flips to the other side
  • Earn sats while you sleep
  • Works in both bull and bear markets
